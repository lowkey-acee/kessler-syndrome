[ 01 — THE MECHANISM ]

A feedback loop, not an accident.

In 1978, NASA scientist Donald Kessler modeled what happens when orbital debris crosses a critical density: each collision creates fragments, fragments raise the odds of further collisions, and the process can sustain itself — without a single new launch.
